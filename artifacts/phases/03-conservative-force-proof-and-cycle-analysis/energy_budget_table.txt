Key observations:

1. W_net = 0.0 EXACTLY for all 6 configurations.
   This is not a numerical coincidence -- it follows from the conservative
   force structure: F(a) depends only on separation a, not on direction
   of travel, so the line integral over any closed cycle vanishes identically.

2. W_close > 0 for all configurations.
   The attractive Casimir force does positive work as plates close (energy
   is extracted from the vacuum/thermal field).

3. W_open = -W_close for all configurations.
   The same attractive force requires exactly the same energy input to
   separate the plates, returning the system to its initial state.

4. Drude and plasma W_close differ by 3.14% at T = 300K.
   The plasma model gives larger |W_close| due to the nonzero TE l=0
   reflection coefficient, which adds to the Casimir force. Both models
   independently give W_net = 0.

5. Thermal correction for ideal plates at 300K is negligible (~10^-7)
   at 100 nm separation, since the thermal wavelength (7.6 um) >> a_min.

6. Energy conservation verified to machine precision for all configurations:
   |W_net - (Delta_E_vac + Q)| / |W_close| = 0.0

CONCLUSION:
  W_net / |W_close| < 10^{-10} for all 6 test configurations, confirming
  that no net energy can be extracted from quasi-static Casimir cycles at
  any temperature for ideal, Drude, or plasma material models. The Casimir
  force is conservative; cyclic processes return zero net work.

  This verifies claim-cycle-work-finite-t and advances project claim
  claim-cycle-work (no ZPE extraction via quasi-static boundary changes).
